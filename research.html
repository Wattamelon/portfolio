Vehicle Type Specific Traffic Volume Analysis Using STGCN with LSTM-Based Residual Correction

A lane-level traffic prediction study that adds LSTM residual correction to STGCN, helping capture temporal prediction errors that remain after the baseline model.

Overview

Predicting traffic conditions by vehicle type

The study uses real-world road traffic data to forecast queue length and average speed for passenger cars, buses, trucks, and motorcycles. STGCN learns road-network and time-based patterns, while the added LSTM module learns residual patterns for a final correction.

Research focus

Rather than replacing STGCN, the proposed model corrects what the baseline misses: STGCN creates a base prediction, and an LSTM models the remaining temporal error before producing the final prediction.

Input & output

Past 12 five-minute intervals (one hour) across 9 input channels are used to predict the next five-minute condition across 8 output channels: queue length and average speed for each vehicle type.

Dataset

Real-world, lane-level traffic data

Traffic was collected across nine intersections in Bucheon City, Gyeonggi-do, South Korea, from August to October 2022. Each lane is represented as a graph node, with physical lane-to-lane connections used as the adjacency matrix.

Results

Lower prediction error than the baseline

Across the overall results, the residual-correction model reduced both MAE and RMSE. Bus and motorcycle traffic had relatively lower volumes, and larger relative improvements were observed for these vehicle types.
